# MCSR Ranked Overworld: Village & Shipwreck Seeds

In **MCSR Ranked** (1.16.1 RSG Filtered Seeds), **Village** and **Shipwreck** are two of the core overworld seed archetypes. Ranked seed filters guarantee viable iron and Nether entry options near spawn, shifting focus to execution speed and resource routing.

---

## 1. Village Seeds

### Resource Priority & Routing
- **Bell & Meeting Point**: The village bell marks the meeting point where the **Iron Golem** spawns. Locate the bell first to track down the golem immediately while gathering wood and beds along the path.
- **Wood & Tools**: Chop 3–4 logs (~12–16 planks) for a crafting table, wooden/stone axe, stone pickaxe, and building blocks.
- **Iron Golem Kill (3–5 Iron + Wood)**:
  - Aggro the golem, pillar up **3 blocks** vertically (or trap it under a 2-high doorway/roof), and crit it out with an axe.
  - Drops **3–5 iron ingots**:
    - **3 Iron**: Craft a **Water Bucket** (3); light the portal using lava + wood planks, or grab extra iron from houses/blacksmith.
    - **4 Iron**: Craft **Bucket** (3) + **Flint and Steel** (1); mine obsidian with a stone pickaxe during portal casting.
    - **5 Iron**: Craft **Bucket** (3) + **Flint and Steel** (1) + **Shovel** (1) for instant flint, or save for an iron pickaxe if paired with chest iron.
- **Blacksmith (Weaponsmith) Chest**:
  - High priority when present. Contains **iron ingots, iron pickaxe/armor, bread/apples, gold, diamonds, and obsidian** (3–7 obsidian enables a fast obsidian portal build).
  - Features **two guaranteed lava source blocks** in the exterior basin—usable for emergency portal casting or wood-lighting.
- **Hay Bales → Bread**:
  - Break hay bales using a **wooden or stone hoe** for rapid harvesting.
  - Craft `Hay Bale → 9 Wheat → 3 Bread` (typically 2–3 bales = 6–9 bread) for high-saturation run food.
- **Beds**:
  - Harvest **6–8+ beds** from houses on the route between the tree, golem, and lava pool for the End One-Cycle and Nether bastion/terrain explosives.

## 2. Shipwreck Seeds

### Chest Order & Identification
Shipwrecks generate up to three chests depending on intact hull sections. Always loot in priority order:
1. **Treasure Chest (Upper Stern / Back Cabin)**: **#1 Priority.** Contains iron ingots, gold ingots, nuggets, emeralds, diamonds, and lapis. In MCSR Ranked Shipwreck seeds, this chest is filtered to guarantee enough iron for Nether entry.
2. **Supply Chest (Bow / Front Lower Deck)**: **#2 Priority.** Contains food (suspicious stew, wheat, carrots, potatoes), coal, TNT, leather armor, and bamboo/paper (for crafting bread/sticks if wood is scarce).
3. **Map Chest (Lower Stern / Back Cabin)**: **Skip** in standard shipwreck seeds (contains buried treasure map, compass, paper, feathers) unless routing into a hybrid Buried Treasure split.

### Loot Utilization
- **Iron & Diamonds**: With **7+ iron** (or 2–3 diamonds), craft an **Iron/Diamond Pickaxe + Bucket + Flint and Steel**. With **4–6 iron**, craft **Bucket + Flint and Steel** and mine cobblestone from the ocean floor or shore for a stone pickaxe.
- **Gold**: Craft **Golden Boots** (4 ingots) immediately to prevent Piglin aggro upon entering the Nether, or hold extra ingots for faster Bastion bartering.
- **Emeralds**: Usually discarded for inventory space unless a coastal village fletcher/cleric is directly on route.
- **Food**: Craft wheat from the supply chest into **Bread**, or use **Suspicious Stew** (provides instant saturation/regen/jump boost, though beware blindness stews). Supplement with kelp or shore animals if needed.

### Portal Entry: Ocean Magma Ravine vs. Lava Pool
| Feature | Ocean Magma Ravine Portal | Surface / Coastal Lava Pool Portal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **When to Use** | Deep ocean spawn, visible magma bubbles nearby, 7+ iron (iron/diamond pickaxe) + doors. | Shore/island spawn, visible surface lava pool, 4–6 iron (stone pickaxe only). |
| **Construction** | Swim to ravine floor (`Y=10–11`), place **doors** for air pockets, cast portal directly into the underwater lava seam (`L`-cast or wall cast). | Standard surface 4-block, dead-bush, or `L`-shape bucket cast using nearby water. |
| **Pros & Cons** | Fastest travel time from ocean shipwrecks and spawns deeper in the Nether; requires careful oxygen management and fast pickaxe speed underwater. | No drowning risk or underwater mining penalty; slower if swimming to land and hunting surface gravel/water takes extra time. |
