# Overworld Seed Types: Buried Treasure, Desert Temple, & Ruined Portal

In MCSR Ranked (Minecraft 1.16.1 RSG filtered seed brackets), Buried Treasure (BT), Desert Temple, and Ruined Portal (RP) represent three of the five core overworld seed archetypes alongside Village and Shipwreck. Each archetype dictates a distinct early-game resource routing and Nether entry strategy.

---

## 1. Buried Treasure (BT)
Buried Treasure seeds spawn the player near a beach or ocean coast with a guaranteed buried chest nearby.

- **Chunk `9, 9` Dig & Mapless Detection:** Buried Treasure chests always generate at chunk-relative coordinates **`X=9, Z=9`** (`Chunk: x 9 z` on the F3 debug screen). Runners locate the chunk via F3+G chunk borders or mapless C-counter / entity spikes (or a nearby shipwreck map) and dig down at `9, 9` beneath sand/gravel—stopping just before sandstone/stone to uncover the chest.
- **Loot Profile:**
  - **Iron & Gold Ingots:** Typically yields enough iron for a bucket, flint & steel, iron pickaxe, and shield/axe, plus gold ingots for Piglin bartering or gold helmet crafting.
  - **Cooked Fish (Cod/Salmon):** High-saturation food source that eliminates the need to hunt surface animals or hay bales.
  - **TNT & Diamonds:** Occasional TNT (useful for wood/block harvesting) and diamonds (for a diamond pickaxe or fast obsidian mining).
- **Wood & Ocean Ravine Portal:**
  - **Wood:** Harvested from nearby trees or a coastal shipwreck before or immediately after looting the BT.
  - **Portal Entry:** Because players already have an iron pickaxe (to break magma blocks) and water/bucket control, the standard play is diving into an **ocean magma ravine** to perform a underwater casting portal at the bottom lava layer (`Y=11`), utilizing air pockets from doors or magma-to-obsidian conversion.

## 2. Desert Temple
Desert Temple seeds spawn the player in or adjacent to a desert biome containing a surface pyramid structure and an accessible lava pool.

- **Pit Entry & TNT Trap Disarm:**
  - Players mine through the center blue terracotta block (or drop down a corner block of the 3x3 shaft) into the hidden chamber.
  - **Disarming:** Immediately break the **stone pressure plate** at the center of the floor before looting or mining the **9 TNT blocks** underneath.
- **4-Chest Loot Table:**
  - **Metals:** Iron and gold ingots (plus occasional diamonds/emeralds) for bucket, flint & steel, pickaxe, and gold armor/bartering.
  - **Gunpowder & Sand:** Combined with extra desert sand to craft additional TNT if needed, while the 9 floor TNT blocks provide instant explosives for Nether bed/TNT bastion routing or One-Cycle dragon fights.
  - **String & Bones:** String is crafted into **wool** (`4 string = 1 wool`) to build beds for the End/Nether, bypassing sheep hunting.
  - **Enchanted Books & Golden Apples:** High-value utility (e.g., Respiration, Protection, Unbreaking) and emergency healing/Piglin pacification.
- **Lava Pool Portal:**
  - After crafting a bucket, flint & steel, and stone/iron tools using sticks from dead bushes or nearby trees, runners route to a nearby **surface desert lava pool** to cast a standard bucket portal and enter the Nether.

## 3. Ruined Portal (RP)
Ruined Portal seeds place the player near a surface or partially buried Nether portal frame with a loot chest and surrounding magma/lava.

- **RP Chest Loot:**
  - **Obsidian:** Often contains `1–6+` regular obsidian blocks, allowing immediate frame completion without bucket-casting.
  - **Crying Obsidian:** Frequently spawns in the chest or frame; cannot form a valid portal and serves only as building/support blocks or respawn anchor material.
  - **Ignition:** **Flint & Steel** or **Fire Charges** provide instant portal lighting without needing gravel or iron.
  - **Gold Tools & Armor:** Enchanted golden pickaxes (often with Efficiency/Unbreaking), axes, hoes, and armor pieces (boots with Soul Speed or helmet to pacify Piglins).
- **Crying Obsidian Replacement & Frame Completion:**
  - Any **Crying Obsidian** occupying the inner 10-block portal frame (excluding corners) must be mined out or bypassed.
  - Since gold pickaxes **cannot** harvest obsidian (they break the block faster than hand/iron if enchanted with Efficiency, or players use lava-casting beside the frame), runners either mine out blocking crying obsidian with a diamond/iron/gold pickaxe to replace it with chest obsidian, or **bucket-cast** fresh obsidian directly into the missing frame slots using the portal's natural lava pools and a nearby water source.
