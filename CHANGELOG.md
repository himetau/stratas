# Changes

What's new in each version, in plain words. The section for a version goes on top of its GitHub release.

## 1.3.0

- A welcome screen the first time you open Stratas: what it looks at and what stays on your computer, your Minecraft name, and a few settings. Same on the website.
- Your name is only what you type. Stratas doesn't guess it from your replays anymore unless you click "Find it in my replays".
- Settings, System, Help: report a problem (copies your version and activity log for a GitHub issue), suggest a feature, and a link to the GitHub page.
- No more rain in the background. Effects are off by default now, snow, embers and stars are still in Settings, Effects.
- The browser version shows its own settings again instead of the desktop app's.

## 1.2.0

- Pick runs from one player: type their Minecraft name on the Find page and it finds their recent wins, at any Elo.
- The Practice page shows the player's Elo in that match and what they gained, so it's easy to spot in their Recent Matches in Ranked. There's a button to copy their name too.
- Profile: the radar compares you with other players near your Elo instead of your own older games.
- "What to practice" instead of habits, in the app and on the share card.
- The profile shows your average win time instead of the median.
- The website, stratas.lol, with the app in the browser. No install needed, you add your replays by hand.

## 1.1.0

- Update button: Stratas checks for new versions and updates in one click.
- Splits straight from Ranked right after a private room run, before the replay is even saved.
- "How practice works" on the Find page.
